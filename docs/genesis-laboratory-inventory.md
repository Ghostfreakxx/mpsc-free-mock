# Tochhawng Virtual Laboratory inventory

The independent laboratory route is `/laboratory`. Existing JEE and NEET class demonstrations remain available in their original lessons.

| Area | Interactive modules shipped on the laboratory route | Remaining scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physics | Ideal projectile motion, small-angle pendulum, and a two-resistor DC circuit. Variable controls, live measurements, reset and practice. Motion also supports playback and time selection. | Newton's laws, gravity, friction, circular motion, waves, circuit extensions, magnetism and optics. Reuse the existing course experiments where appropriate. |
| Chemistry | Particle-count model for isotopes and ions, including changes to mass number and charge. | Geometry, bonding, periodicity, electron configurations, reactions, acids and bases, organic structures. |
| Biology | Mendelian pea-seed colour with adjustable parent genotypes, a Punnett table and predicted ratios. | Cells, anatomy, other inheritance patterns, photosynthesis, division and ecosystems. Review existing diagrams before adding new assets. |
| Mathematics | Quadratic graphs with adjustable vertex, curvature and direction, and computed real roots. | Other functions, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, calculus, probability, statistics, vectors and coordinate geometry. |
| Political science and history | Illustrative 100-seat assembly with adjustable yes and no votes and a live simple-majority threshold. Constitution interaction remains in the study room. | Constitutional institutions, political theory, history and Mizoram timelines. |
| Geography and economics | Same-meridian latitude separation and spherical surface distance. | Terrain, weather, maps and regional geography, demographics, markets, inflation, growth and budgets. |

The current motion models assume constant gravity and equal launch and landing heights for projectiles, and small angles and negligible friction for the pendulum. The duration, range, and period are tested numerically. Student controls have keyboard alternatives and reduced motion uses manual time control. Connect new modules to reviewed course notes and questions and retain a text equivalent for each visualization.

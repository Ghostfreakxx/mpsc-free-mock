# Tochhawng Virtual Laboratory inventory

The independent laboratory route is `/laboratory`. Existing JEE and NEET class demonstrations remain available in their original lessons.

| Area | Interactive modules shipped on the laboratory route | Remaining scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physics | Ideal projectile motion and small-angle pendulum. Variable sliders, time slider, playback, reset, live measurements and a practice question. | Newton's laws, gravity, friction, circular motion, waves, circuits, magnetism and optics. Reuse the existing course experiments where appropriate. |
| Chemistry | — | Atomic structure, geometry, bonding, periodicity, configurations, reactions, acids and bases, organic structures. |
| Biology | — | Cells, anatomy, DNA and inheritance, photosynthesis, division and ecosystems. Review existing diagrams before adding new assets. |
| Mathematics | — | Functions, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, calculus, probability, statistics, vectors and coordinate geometry. |
| Political science and history | Constitution interaction remains available in the study room. | Constitutional institutions, political theory, history and Mizoram timelines. |
| Geography and economics | — | Terrain, weather, maps and regional geography, demographics, markets, inflation, growth and budgets. |

The current motion models assume constant gravity and equal launch and landing heights for projectiles, and small angles and negligible friction for the pendulum. The duration, range, and period are tested numerically. Student controls have keyboard alternatives and reduced motion uses manual time control. Connect new modules to reviewed course notes and questions and retain a text equivalent for each visualization.

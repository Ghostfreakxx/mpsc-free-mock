# Kinetics and Practical Chemistry expansion

This eight-topic batch was initially held as a draft on 30 October 2026 after the narration credential expired. After the local key was updated, the recording process was corrected to use it instead of the stale inherited credential, and the material was integrated with the recorded classes.

The batch adds eight narrated chapters, sixteen original MCQs, six worked examples, matching study sections, sources and two diagrams. It is additional practice, not exhaustive syllabus coverage or official past-paper content.

## Kinetics

- Independent concentration changes resolve ambiguous partial orders.
- Collision energy and conditional orientation filters distinguish event counts from rate constants.
- A zero-order law has a depletion boundary and a stipulated feed resets the initial state.
- A supporting two-population model explains why a total signal need not have one fixed half-life.

Class totals: 30 narrated chapters, 33 written sections, 25 worked examples and 58 questions.

## Practical Chemistry

- Nickel dimethylglyoxime confirmation includes red chelation, ligand stoichiometry and donor counts.
- Calcium oxalate confirmation includes protonation and free-ion solubility thresholds.
- Calcium/barium flame comparisons include sodium contamination and reagent blanks.
- Orthophosphate molybdate confirmation distinguishes the qualitative response from a calibrated total-phosphorus assay.

Class totals: 33 narrated chapters, 33 written sections, 21 worked examples and 56 questions. Descriptions interpret supplied evidence rather than provide an unsupervised experimental protocol.

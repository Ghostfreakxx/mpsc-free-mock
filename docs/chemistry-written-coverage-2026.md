# Chemistry written coverage: JEE Main 2026

Baseline: [official NTA syllabus](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/document/syllabus-2026/).

This is a coverage inventory, not a claim that the course is complete.
There are 22 authored written lessons spanning all 20 Chemistry units.
This means each unit has at least one foundation, not exhaustive topic coverage.
Organic Principles, Coordination Compounds, Chemical Kinetics, Chemical Thermodynamics
and Solutions each have a five-chapter Marin
recording with timed captions and chapter visuals. Other Chemistry lessons
remain written-only. Existing
Physics recordings and captions have not been regenerated.

## Remaining course work

Chemical Thermodynamics and Solutions now each have five narrated chapters,
aligned captions, visual boards and six original quiz questions. Their written
companions retain three worked examples each. Recordings are available at
`/jee/classes/chemical-thermodynamics` and `/jee/classes/solutions`.
Detailed entropy integrals, enthalpy cycles, phase diagrams and nonideal activity
calculations remain beyond these foundations.

Equilibrium now includes seven worked examples and twelve original MCQs,
including buffer neutralization, salt hydrolysis, dilution before precipitation
and a weak-acid calculation that requires the quadratic rather than a shortcut.
These are written practice additions, not a prerecorded Chemistry class.

Electrochemistry now has ten sections, seven worked examples and twelve MCQs.
The additions cover basic-medium permanganate reduction, conductivity units,
weak-electrolyte ionization, lead-acid discharge and current efficiency.

Chemical kinetics now includes seven worked examples and twelve MCQs, with
pressure-based first-order measurements, depletion boundaries, mixed rate-law
changes and successive half-lives. A five-chapter recorded class accompanies these notes
at `/jee/classes/chemical-kinetics`.

No unit is missing a written foundation. Detailed subtopic coverage, additional
worked problems, independent factual review and Chemistry narration still need work.

An existing foundation does not mean exhaustive unit coverage. The `remaining`
field of each lesson identifies further practice or content required.
Do not relabel these notes as prerecorded classes until audio, aligned captions
and player integration have actually been generated and validated.

Coordination Compounds now includes seven worked examples and twelve original MCQs,
covering outer-sphere chloride precipitation, mixed-ligand naming, nickel(II)
geometry, fac/mer arrangements and pairing-energy comparisons for octahedral d6.
A five-chapter recorded class now accompanies these notes at
`/jee/classes/coordination-compounds`; detailed stereochemical drawings and extended
stability and reaction-sequence practice remain outstanding.

Organic Principles now includes seven worked examples and twelve original MCQs,
with branched alcohol naming, pKa comparisons, substitution electron bookkeeping
and direct allylic resonance. Further multifunctional naming, stereochemical
drawings and solvent-dependent acidity practice remain. A five-chapter recorded
class now accompanies this written lesson at `/jee/classes/organic-principles`.

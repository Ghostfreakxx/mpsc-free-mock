# Chemistry written coverage: JEE Main 2026

Baseline: [official NTA syllabus](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/document/syllabus-2026/).

This is a coverage inventory, not a claim that the course is complete.
There are 22 authored written lessons spanning all 20 Chemistry units.
This means each unit has at least one foundation, not exhaustive topic coverage.
Organic Principles has a five-chapter Marin recording. Coordination Compounds,
Chemical Kinetics, Chemical Thermodynamics and Solutions each have ten chapters.
Equilibrium has eight
chapters and Redox and Electrochemistry has nine. Atomic Structure and Chemical
Bonding each have eight chapters. Mole Concept and Periodic Trends also have
eight chapters each. Hydrocarbons and Organic Halogens each have eight chapters.
Organic Oxygen has ten chapters and Organic Nitrogen has eight.
Biomolecules, Purification and Analysis, and Practical Chemistry each have eight chapters.
P-block and D/F-block each have ten chapters. All twenty recordings include
timed captions and chapter visuals. Every Chemistry unit has a recorded foundation.
Chemistry Foundations and Atomic Spectra retain written material without separate recordings. Existing
Physics recordings and captions have not been regenerated.

## Remaining course work

P-block now has ten narrated chapters at `/jee/classes/p-block`, covering groups
13–18, first-member differences, bonding, hydrides and worked oxygen oxidation
numbers. D/F-block has ten chapters at `/jee/classes/d-f-block`, including actual
ion configurations, variable states, colour, magnetism, solid structures, oxidants,
contraction and worked magnetic/redox calculations. Each includes twelve original
questions. All twenty Chemistry units now have at least one recorded foundation;
this does not establish exhaustive syllabus coverage. Further datasets, detailed
structures, preparation schemes and extended synthesis/experimental practice remain.

Biomolecules now has eight narrated chapters at `/jee/classes/biomolecules`,
covering sugars, hydrolysis, polymers, peptides, protein structure, enzymes,
vitamin/hormone classification and nucleic acids. Purification and Analysis has
eight chapters at `/jee/classes/purification-analysis`, including separation
principles, chromatography, elemental evidence and composition calculations.
Practical Chemistry has eight chapters at `/jee/classes/practical-chemistry`,
covering titrations, redox ratios, qualitative evidence, preparation principles,
calorimetry and clock-rate interpretation. Each has ten original questions.
Detailed stereochemistry, full analytical schemes and larger datasets remain.

Organic Oxygen now has ten narrated chapters at `/jee/classes/organic-oxygen`,
covering functional groups, alcohol oxidation, phenol, carbonyl addition and
reduction, aldol and Cannizzaro, iodoform, carboxylic acids, ether cleavage and
Grignard carbon accounting. Organic Nitrogen has eight chapters at
`/jee/classes/organic-nitrogen`, including classification, solvent-dependent
basicity, preparation, identification, diazotization, replacement and azo coupling.
Each has ten original questions. Further detailed mechanisms, stereochemical
drawings and multistep synthesis remain for additional lessons.

Hydrocarbons now has eight narrated chapters at `/jee/classes/hydrocarbons`,
including unsaturation, radical chains, reagent-specific addition, reductive
ozonolysis, acetylides, selective reduction and aromatic substitution. Organic
Halogens has eight chapters at `/jee/classes/organic-halogens`, covering reaction
centres, preparation, SN1/SN2, elimination, activated haloarenes, Grignard quenching,
coupling and compound properties. Each includes ten original questions. Detailed
stereochemical drawings, alkyne hydration and longer synthesis problems remain.

Mole Concept now has eight narrated chapters at `/jee/classes/mole-concept`,
covering chemical laws, particle and mass conversions, composition, formulas,
limiting reactants, purity and yield, gas volumes and dilution. Periodic Trends
has eight chapters at `/jee/classes/periodic-trends`, including shielding,
radius definitions, isoelectronic comparisons, ionization exceptions, successive
ionization, electron gain, electronegativity and oxide behaviour. Each includes
ten original questions. Longer reaction chains, combustion analysis and detailed
group chemistry remain for further work.

Atomic Structure now has an eight-chapter recording at `/jee/classes/atomic-structure`,
combining particle counts, photon calculations, Bohr relations, spectra, matter
waves, quantum numbers, nodes and electron configurations. The Atomic Structure
and Atomic Spectra written lessons provide companion practice. Chemical Bonding
has an eight-chapter recording at `/jee/classes/chemical-bonding`, including
polarization, electron counts, resonance, five- and six-domain VSEPR examples,
polarity, sigma/pi counting, molecular orbitals and hydrogen bonding.
Each new recording includes twelve original questions. Detailed radial plots,
quantitative lattice cycles and fuller orbital diagrams remain for further work.

Chemical Thermodynamics and Solutions now each have ten narrated chapters,
aligned captions, visual boards and twelve original quiz questions. Their written
companions each have twelve sections and six worked examples. Thermodynamics adds
formation-data arithmetic, a lattice-dissociation/hydration cycle, vessel-inclusive
calorimetry, phase-transition and constant-Cp entropy, and system/surroundings
entropy balances. Solutions adds density conversion, Henry's-law conventions,
nonideal mixtures and azeotropes, boiling-elevation molar mass, dissociation and
isotonic comparisons. Recordings are available at
`/jee/classes/chemical-thermodynamics` and `/jee/classes/solutions`.
Full Born-Haber cycles, variable heat-capacity integrals, detailed phase diagrams
and quantitative nonideal activities remain beyond this coverage.

Equilibrium now includes seven worked examples and twelve original MCQs,
including buffer neutralization, salt hydrolysis, dilution before precipitation
and a weak-acid calculation that requires the quadratic rather than a shortcut.
An eight-chapter recorded class now accompanies these notes at
`/jee/classes/equilibrium`, covering dynamic equilibrium, Q and K, pressure and
temperature changes, acid-base models, weak-acid calculations, buffers,
hydrolysis and precipitation after dilution.

Electrochemistry now has ten sections, seven worked examples and twelve MCQs.
The additions cover basic-medium permanganate reduction, conductivity units,
weak-electrolyte ionization, lead-acid discharge and current efficiency.
A nine-chapter recorded class accompanies these notes at
`/jee/classes/redox-electrochemistry`, including the additional conductance,
charge-balance and current-efficiency calculations.

Chemical Kinetics now includes thirteen written sections, ten worked examples
and eighteen original MCQs. Its ten-chapter recording at
`/jee/classes/chemical-kinetics` adds stoichiometric rate normalization,
pseudo-first-order conditions, model-testing plots, high completion percentages
and labelled catalyst energy profiles. Further mixed datasets and mechanism
practice remain; second-order integrated laws are not claimed as required scope.

No unit is missing a written foundation. Detailed subtopic coverage, additional
worked problems, independent factual review and Chemistry narration still need work.

An existing foundation does not mean exhaustive unit coverage. The `remaining`
field of each lesson identifies further practice or content required.
Do not relabel these notes as prerecorded classes until audio, aligned captions
and player integration have actually been generated and validated.

Coordination Compounds now includes thirteen written sections, ten worked examples and eighteen original MCQs,
covering outer-sphere chloride precipitation, mixed-ligand naming, nickel(II)
geometry, fac/mer arrangements and pairing-energy comparisons for octahedral d6.
A ten-chapter recorded class now accompanies these notes at
`/jee/classes/coordination-compounds`, adding ionization/linkage distinctions,
labelled fac/mer positions, chelation and optical-isomer concepts, stepwise/overall
formation constants and analytical/biological applications. Detailed optical-isomer
models and quantitative extraction/analysis sequences remain outstanding.

Organic Principles now includes seven worked examples and twelve original MCQs,
with branched alcohol naming, pKa comparisons, substitution electron bookkeeping
and direct allylic resonance. Further multifunctional naming, stereochemical
drawings and solvent-dependent acidity practice remain. A five-chapter recorded
class now accompanies this written lesson at `/jee/classes/organic-principles`.

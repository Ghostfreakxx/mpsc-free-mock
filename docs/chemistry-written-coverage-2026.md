# Chemistry coverage: JEE Main 2026

Baseline: [official NTA syllabus](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/document/syllabus-2026/).

All twenty Chemistry units have a recorded foundation with Marin narration,
aligned English captions, chapter visuals, original practice and downloadable notes.
This is a coverage inventory, not a claim of exhaustive syllabus coverage.
There are 22 written Chemistry lessons: the twenty companions below, plus Chemistry
Foundations and Atomic Spectra, which retain additional written material without
separate recordings. Existing Physics recordings have not been regenerated.

## Latest expansion

P-Block adds phase-dependent phosphorus pentachloride species, two chlorine disproportionation ledgers, donor-specific Lewis-adduct enthalpy comparisons and ideal silicate oxygen-sharing counts. The silicate diagram is a connectivity graph, not a planar molecular geometry.

D/F-Block adds linked dichromate iodometry, acidic permanganate–oxalate stoichiometry and autocatalysis, cerium/europium redox and supporting density/molar-volume comparisons. Indirect assay conditions and original numerical datasets are explicit.

Together this batch adds eight narrated chapters, sixteen original MCQs and six worked examples, with matching written companions and recorded practice.

## Remaining scope

The individual companions retain their specific remaining-work statements. Further
reaction networks, fuller excited-orbital derivations, detailed heteronuclear orbital mixing,
nonideal equilibrium and solution datasets, detailed group-specific comparisons,
broader actinoid configurations, extended elemental analysis, nonideal extraction,
complete systematic ion analysis and broader organic synthesis practice remain.
P-block compounds illustrate the official trend-focused scope; inclusion is not a
claim that every historical preparation is required by the current syllabus.

## References and publication checks

The notes reference OpenStax phosphorus/silicate and transition-metal teaching, Purdue halogen chemistry, primary boron-adduct and permanganate–oxalate studies, CSU Dominguez Hills iodometry notes and IChO preparatory analysis problems. Numerical datasets and MCQs in this batch are original practice.

Do not relabel written-only material as recorded until audio, aligned captions
and player integration have been generated and validated. Required release checks
include content tests, lint, types, dependency audit, production build, course and
library browser checks, and the full mobile smoke test. Scope labels must remain
honest about outstanding work.

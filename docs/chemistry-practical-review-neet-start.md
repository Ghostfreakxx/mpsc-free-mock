# Chemistry practical review and NEET start

Reviewed 4 November 2026 against JEE Main 2027 Paper 1, Chemistry Unit 20. Chapter numbers below are one-based. Interpretations concern examination evidence, not unsupervised laboratory recipes.

The previous 41-chapter practical recording named all six preparations but did not adequately explain their chemistry. Ten appended chapters now cover those gaps and strengthen prescribed analytical/experimental principles. The original 41 chapters and 72 questions stay unchanged; the class now has 51 chapters, 92 original questions and 30 worked examples in its written companion. New narration and captions accompany all additions.

This closes the confirmed explanatory gaps found in this practical review. A complete multication interference scheme and wider mixed datasets remain practice extensions. This review does not certify every subheading in the other 53 JEE units or JEE Advanced. All 54 JEE Main units already have recorded foundations; do not confuse that milestone with exhaustive examination preparation.

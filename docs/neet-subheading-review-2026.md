# NEET Physics and Chemistry detailed subheading review

Checked 5 November 2026 against the official NEET UG 2026 syllabus. 189 grouped subheading entries across 40 units.

Human review of grouped prescribed subheadings against actual narration, formulas/steps and worked evidence. References retain original chapter numbering. Existing explanation means foundational explanatory evidence was located, not examination mastery. Supplemented identifies a group with an added targeted class. Field performance, wider diagrams and mixed/timed practice remain.

## Confirmed teaching additions

Physics: formal unit-vector notation/normalization, component algebra and scalar/vector products. Applications of products existed in other classes, but did not replace this foundation.

Chemistry: explicit calcium-carbide ethyne preparation, alkyne chain/cyclic polymerisation, named Freon-12 uses, sawhorse/Newman conversion, transition-element occurrence, representative oxygen-compound applications, phenol sulphonation, alkyne halogen/HX equivalents, homologous series and combining gas volumes. Some general headings allow multiple examples; this is not a demand for every possible preparation, mineral or industrial use.

## False gaps rejected

Bulk/shear moduli and stress–strain distinctions are in Materials Applications 1; Huygens ray-law derivation is in Interference 2; physical phase equilibria/Henry are in Equilibrium 1; multistage ionisation is in Equilibrium 13/23/30; practical preparations are in Practical Chemistry 42–51. DDT is spoken as separate letters. Carbon tetrachloride is not in this NEET 2026 organohalogen list. Do not duplicate these lessons or infer gaps from missing exact words.

## Limits and maintenance

This is a documented foundation-level review, not a guarantee that every possible problem or hands-on observation is mastered. Required headings are grouped when one explanation supports related clauses; the published evidence list makes those groupings reviewable. Existing material is reused without removing JEE extensions. Each evidence reference records a fingerprint of its narration/steps so changes trigger a new review in tests. NEET and JEE progress keys remain separate. No source keyword hit automatically assigned reviewed status.

Next: additional labelled diagrams, authentic experimental data, mixed/NEET-timed practice and factual peer review can improve depth. These remain practice/review work rather than automatic evidence that a foundation topic is missing.

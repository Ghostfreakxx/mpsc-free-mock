# Content publication policy

Published practice is the source-checked, original foundation set in `app/data/reviewed-content.ts`. It is not a comprehensive exam bank, an official PYQ collection, or a prediction of examination content. Review date: 2026-09-27. Review was AI-assisted, not certification by an examination body or independent subject expert.

The second release added 200 practice slots to each of the four exam streams, and MPSC was later expanded further. Current totals (2026-10-21): MPSC 678, NEET 582, JEE 520 and CUET PG 384. Some questions are shared across streams: the 2,164 stream slots come from 1,480 unique questions, 684 of which appear in more than one stream, and they are not 1,480 distinct concepts. Additions include deterministic numerical variants and paired recall/matching variants within the existing foundation topics. Their source locations explicitly identify those variants. The larger count does not expand this into full-syllabus coverage. Generation is deterministic and does not call an AI service at runtime. Question IDs from the first release are retained.

The JEE foundation extension (2026-09-29) adds 99 hand-written questions in eleven topics defined in `app/data/jee-topics.ts`: Physics (constant-acceleration kinematics, projectile motion, work-energy theorem), Chemistry (mole concept, molarity and dilution, gas laws) and Mathematics (arithmetic and geometric sequences, logarithms, permutations and combinations, differentiation rules), taking JEE to 308 published questions. Each item cites an OpenStax section; worked answers for representative numericals are recomputed in `scripts/content.test.mjs`. Review was AI-assisted, as above.

The NEET foundation extension (2026-09-29) publishes the six JEE Physics and Chemistry foundation topics (65 questions) to NEET as well, and adds 68 hand-written Biology questions in seven topics defined in `app/data/neet-topics.ts`: cell cycle and mitosis, meiosis, DNA structure, DNA replication models, Mendelian inheritance, photosynthesis and enzymes. Each cites an OpenStax Biology 2e section. NEET Biology grows from 70 to 138 questions, enough for a full-length 45/45/90 paper in the exam simulator. Review was AI-assisted, as above.

The high-yield extension (2026-09-29) adds 109 original questions in twelve topics (`app/data/high-yield-topics.ts`) chosen by the triangulated pattern scan in `PATTERN-SCAN.md`: current electricity, modern physics and coordination compounds for JEE and NEET; determinants, vectors and the binomial theorem for JEE; digestion, gas exchange and circulation for NEET; and Union executive, Parliament and emergency provisions for CUET PG. The polity topic was later published to MPSC in English, with its Mizo translation pending (see below). Review was AI-assisted, as above.

The second pattern-scan batch (2026-10-20) adds 184 original questions in twenty topics (`app/data/pattern-batch-topics.ts`), taken from the "Still open" list in `PATTERN-SCAN.md`. They cover electrostatics, thin lenses, the first law of thermodynamics, Faraday's law, periodic trends, enthalpy and Hess's law, and hydrocarbons for JEE and NEET. For JEE they add definite integrals, separable differential equations, and lines and planes in 3D. For NEET they add energy flow in ecosystems, human reproduction, evolution, the kidney, and plant water transport. CUET PG gains price elasticity, social stratification, and panchayats and municipalities. MPSC and CUET PG gain percentages and simple interest. Each item cites an OpenStax section or the Constitution text, and worked answers for representative numericals are recomputed in `scripts/content.test.mjs`. The 26 MPSC items have templated draft Mizo translations, which still await fluent-speaker review. The panchayat topic was later published to MPSC in English, with its Mizo translation pending (see below). The OpenStax links match published section titles, but they could not be opened from the build environment. Review was AI-assisted, as above.

The third batch (2026-10-21) adds 201 original questions in twenty-one topics (`app/data/batch-three-topics.ts`), weighted towards MPSC Paper I. Most MPSC general studies is now published in English first. Each topic is listed in `mizoPendingTopicIds`, and the practice page labels it "Mizo translation pending". It never gets a guessed translation; a fluent writer should add the Mizo drafts.
- **MPSC general studies:** the Union judiciary, public finance in the Constitution, the Sixth Schedule and Mizoram's district councils, the making of the Constitution, Gandhi and the national movement, the revolt of 1857, India's location, climate agreements, the Convention on Biological Diversity, and vitamins and minerals. The existing Union executive and panchayat topics now also reach MPSC.
- **MPSC and CUET PG aptitude:** averages, ratio and proportion, and discount, tax and commission. These have templated draft Mizo translations.
- **JEE and NEET:** magnetic force on moving charges, equilibrium constants, cell potentials and wave motion.
- **JEE:** probability and complex numbers.
- **NEET:** neurons, biotechnology tools, and vitamins and minerals.

Constitutional items cite the May 2024 edition. The history and geography items cite NCERT chapters by name through the NCERT textbook portal, because the build environment could not reach the PDFs to confirm file codes. The climate and biodiversity items cite the UNFCCC and CBD secretariats. Worked answers for representative numericals are recomputed in `scripts/content.test.mjs`. Review was AI-assisted, as above.

The original 3,291 question records are preserved verbatim as structured data in `archive/` (MPSC 931, NEET 515, JEE 601, CUET PG 1,244). They are excluded from scored practice while provenance and factual answers await review. Legacy college notes remain visible with a review-pending notice. Historical device responses are retained; current scores include only published questions.

For each new question, check the wording, the unique correct option, every distractor, the explanation and the precise reference location. Record the source URL, review date, stable ID and intended stream. Do not infer verified status from a successful schema test or a working URL.

Official PYQs require the original paper, year/session/shift, question identifier, final official key and matching key identifier. Provisional, dropped and disputed items must not silently enter scored practice. No current published item is claimed as an official PYQ.

Downloads are self-contained, printable HTML foundation packs with original summaries, self-checks, answer reasoning and source links. They work without a network after download; external references need a connection. They are not complete course notes or PDFs. Constitutional references use the explicitly named May 2024 edition. Review legal and syllabus changes before expanding coverage.

Before publication run `npm run validate:banks`, `npm run test:question-rotation`, `npm run test:content`, `npm run lint` and `npm run build`. Review source accuracy separately and test first-answer locking, filters, empty search, each download, narrow screens and offline assets. Automated checks do not establish factual truth.
